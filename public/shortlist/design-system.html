One semantic token set, two platform modes. Everything your friends told you to watch — surfaced the moment you sit down, on the 10-foot TV and in your hand.

06

Principles

The same rules govern both platforms — they keep the product feeling like one product.

Do
  • Reserve burnt-orange for the one thing a person should do next.
  • Let Bitter carry titles; never set body or UI labels in serif.
  • Keep one primary action per screen — Watch or Subscribe, never both as primary.
  • Drive color from people: a recommender keeps their hashed avatar color everywhere.
Don’t
  • Don’t add a second accent hue — the palette is intentionally single-accent.
  • Don’t shrink mobile tap targets below 48pt.
  • Don’t use the cream focus ring on Mobile — focus is a TV/remote affordance.
  • Don’t place text on backdrop art without the hero scrim beneath it.
